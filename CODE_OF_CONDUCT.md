# Code of Conduct

Sketchcast contributors are expected to be respectful, constructive, and
welcoming. Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, doxxing, and sharing
private information without permission are not acceptable.

Focus review comments on the work, explain tradeoffs clearly, and assume good
intent while still protecting users. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful
content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed.

Report conduct concerns privately to `easyroadup@gmail.com`.
